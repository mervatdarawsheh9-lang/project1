Infectious Diseases

1. Black Spot Fungus

Fungal disease causing circular black spots on rose leaves. Thrives in high humidity, leads to leaf drop. Treatment: Fungicides and pruning infected leaves.

2. Rust Disease

Orange powdery fungal infection on leaves. Spreads by wind and tools. Reduces photosynthesis. Treatment: Improve air circulation and fungicides.

3. Bacterial Leaf Spot

Small water-soaked spots turning brown. Spreads by water splash. Causes leaf holes. Treatment: Copper sprays and good ventilation.
